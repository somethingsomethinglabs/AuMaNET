## Connected ridge

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: minimalist logo concept board for AuMaNET. Target dimensions: square 1024 x 1024. Create a polished, restrained, vector-friendly flat graphic on a pure white background. ONE design direction only, called Connected ridge. Product: Australian off-grid 4WD/camping crew communications—local voice, messages and member positions, autonomous radio relaying, internet optional. Meaning: staying connected across terrain. Design ONE memorable compact symbol made of a single substantial continuous forest-green (#164b32) angular band forming two low uneven ridge peaks joined by an intentional generous valley and a subtle connecting lower return. Simplify hard: broad clear negative space, slightly softened corners, confident stroke, distinctive joined geometry. It should feel like a continuous connection across a ridge rather than stock alpine mountain line art. No dots, no triangle mesh, no wireless arcs, no antennas, no map pin, no speech bubble, no Australia silhouette. Preserve the reference app's calm forest-green identity and simplicity, but create a NEW original mark. The reference screenshot is brand context only: do not draw the app or copy its generic mountain icon. Composition: large isolated symbol in upper half; spacious smaller horizontal lockup of the exact same symbol beside exact wordmark "AuMaNET" in a clean carefully spaced humanist sans-serif in lower middle; one modest small black version of the same symbol near bottom to show silhouette. Every specimen must be identical geometry. Pure white board, ample whitespace. No extra descriptive headings or labels, no slogan, no card borders, no UI, no shadows, no gradients, no texture, no photography, no mockup scenery. Refine optical balance like a professional identity designer. Keep mark simple enough to aim for 24px use; do not add miniature ornament.
