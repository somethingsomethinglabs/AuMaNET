Guides/Project and reference

Builds and tested scope

The Android guides describe testing revision 54d4901. The revision is the integrated testing baseline at the time of review. The separate brand refresh is review work.

What we are building

The committed app has Map, Messages, Voice, Group and Network tabs. It includes saved vector map areas, group text and photos, consented shared positions, native group voice and optional shared IPv4 internet. See Android controls.

Where development stands

Development records cover a Samsung Z Flip3 and Nothing Phone (3a) Pro with two XIAO ESP32-S3/HaLow nodes. Results apply to the recorded configurations. Later owner-side work on radio settings and updates has a narrower scope. The test evidence and verification records describe what was exercised.

Screenshots and provenance

Earlier Android captures came from an on-device review batch dated 15 November 2026. The exact installed APK revision for those images is unverified, so the current guides use source-checked control names and the home page illustrates the connection path. The brand handover screenshots show an independent fictional design prototype.

Testing limits

  • Two nodes exercise a direct link. Autonomous relay through another node and larger groups need their own acceptance.
  • Link-test throughput and radio observations apply to the test conditions. Phone GPS supplies the separation estimate.
  • Range, battery endurance, broad Android and headset compatibility and enclosure durability need further evidence.
  • Internet sharing uses an explicitly chosen available donor, with the recorded IPv4 scope.

Source boundary

The public site and GitHub firmware Nightlies are available separately. Android review APKs are Actions artifacts tied to a build run. No supported consumer Android build or complete kit is documented.
