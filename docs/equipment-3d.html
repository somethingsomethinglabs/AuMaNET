Hardware fit status

The source build uses a Raspberry Pi 4 + WM1302 adapter + Wio WM6108 and a separate LoRa subsystem. Our Pi 4 is the shared platform; our Heltec HAT/HC01P V2 is a different stack. Its fit is unverified. The source battery case and twist lock are references; AuMaNET battery selection remains TBD.

No fictional hardware envelopes or calculated tolerance claims are overlaid. Inspect the source mounting features and connector openings before adapting them.
