## What you need

For the tested arrangement, use two provisioned XIAO ESP32-S3 and XIAO HaLow nodes with the matching roles, correct antennas and reliable power. Each participant uses an Android phone with the matching app build. Check [phones and hardware](compatibility.md) before choosing equipment.

Ask the person who prepared the equipment for your assigned node, exact app build and group access. Initial trust setup depends on the provisioned build. The project has yet to publish an accepted clean-board enrollment procedure or a supported consumer kit.

## Preparation order

1. Confirm your app build, node firmware and assigned node.
2. [Connect a provisioned node](initial-configuration.md). Check another member is reachable and exchange a short message.
3. [Save and check offline maps](maps-location.md) while internet is available.
4. Use the [trip checklist](first-trip.md) with the people and equipment you’ll take.
