Guides/Project and reference

Licensing & privacy

Project material

Original project material remains all rights reserved pending licence selection. A public repository, website or contribution invitation does not grant an open-source licence. Imported CAD, libraries and other third-party material retain their own licences and attribution.

Check the individual asset/reference licence before reusing or redistributing it. In particular, a linked community case is not automatically part of the project's redistributable print set. See the CAD source guide.

User data and test artifacts

The application can hold group messages, photos, local maps, positions and device/group identifiers. Explicit sharing/participation controls should be understood per actual build. This page is a documentation summary, not a complete privacy policy or independent audit.

Review diagnostic exports before sharing. Keep signing keys, invitations, provisioning material, raw flash backups, private trip tracks and personal logs out of public commits. See Logs & diagnostics.

Radio and hardware

The AU firmware profile and configured power setting are implementation boundaries, not a certification claim. The exact hardware, antenna, firmware and operating conditions need regulatory review. No guaranteed range, IP rating, battery life or emergency-service availability is declared.

Policies still required

The owner needs to select the project licence and contribution terms, publish an app privacy/data-retention policy, define a private vulnerability-reporting contact and establish release/support and regulatory documentation. Those items are tracked in the documentation roadmap. No contact address or legal approval has been invented here.
