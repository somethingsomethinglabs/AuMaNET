Their claims of “current”, purchasing restrictions, component choices and build/release checklists describe that earlier baseline. They are not instructions for the current ESP32-S3/HaLow investigation.

Use the current handbook, capabilities, decisions and compatibility matrix for the active project. No prior battery, image, RF setting, purchasing-source restriction or enclosure choice carries forward automatically.
