Image Gen prompts

Mode: built-in Image Gen. Reference image for every call: output/playwright/after-map.png.

Connected ridge

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: minimalist logo concept board for AuMaNET. Target dimensions: square 1024 x 1024. Create a polished, restrained, vector-friendly flat graphic on a pure white background. ONE design direction only, called Connected ridge. Product: Australian off-grid 4WD/camping crew communications—local voice, messages and member positions, autonomous radio relaying, internet optional. Meaning: staying connected across terrain. Design ONE memorable compact symbol made of a single substantial continuous forest-green (#164b32) angular band forming two low uneven ridge peaks joined by an intentional generous valley and a subtle connecting lower return. Simplify hard: broad clear negative space, slightly softened corners, confident stroke, distinctive joined geometry. It should feel like a continuous connection across a ridge rather than stock alpine mountain line art. No dots, no triangle mesh, no wireless arcs, no antennas, no map pin, no speech bubble, no Australia silhouette. Preserve the reference app's calm forest-green identity and simplicity, but create a NEW original mark. The reference screenshot is brand context only: do not draw the app or copy its generic mountain icon. Composition: large isolated symbol in upper half; spacious smaller horizontal lockup of the exact same symbol beside exact wordmark "AuMaNET" in a clean carefully spaced humanist sans-serif in lower middle; one modest small black version of the same symbol near bottom to show silhouette. Every specimen must be identical geometry. Pure white board, ample whitespace. No extra descriptive headings or labels, no slogan, no card borders, no UI, no shadows, no gradients, no texture, no photography, no mockup scenery. Refine optical balance like a professional identity designer. Keep mark simple enough to aim for 24px use; do not add miniature ornament.

Paired trail

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: minimalist identity concept board for AuMaNET. Target dimensions: square 1024 x 1024. ONE independent design direction called Paired trail. Product is an Australian off-grid 4WD/camping crew communicator: local voice/messages/member positions, people staying together beyond cellular coverage. Create a NEW compact original symbol from TWO substantial rounded geometric ribbon-like paths moving alongside one another and sharing one simple bridging gesture or generous common negative-space clearing. Symbol should convey companions travelling together and remaining connected, not road lane markings. A gentle purposeful forward bend, slightly offset equal-weight forms, minimal components and distinctive compact silhouette. Optically balanced, neither a pause icon nor two disconnected parallel slashes. Two forms represent togetherness, not literal fixed group size. Flat single forest-green #164b32 on pure white; no gradients. Reference screenshot is existing brand context (forest green, calm/simple), not an app to redesign and not a mountain to copy. Absolutely no mountain, tree, car, footprints, map pin, wifi arcs, antenna, graph nodes, circles of dots, badge or busy monogram. Presentation: one large isolated mark in upper half; smaller horizontal lockup below with exact SAME mark to left of exact wordmark "AuMaNET" in carefully spaced humanist sans-serif; one modest small black specimen of identical symbol near bottom. All specimens repeat the one design faithfully. Pure white background, generous whitespace. No descriptive text/headings/slogan, no page numbers, no UI screenshot/device frame, no gradients/shadows/texture/photographic mockups. Aim at a strong 24px app symbol and one-colour sticker. Do not add details merely to explain every function.

Open link

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: minimalist identity concept board for AuMaNET. Target dimensions square 1024 x 1024, pure white background. ONE new original design direction called Open link. AuMaNET is a calm practical Australian off-grid crew communicator, connecting people locally for group voice/messages/member positions, with no dependence on cellular internet. Design a compact strong symbol from TWO equally weighted interlocking OPEN geometric forms, soft squarish C-like bends, creating ONE generous shared central negative-space clearing. Convey reciprocal peer connection and a group held together. A subtle angular route character is welcome, but no literal terrain. Keep forms substantial, economical, confidently balanced and more distinctive than a generic chain link. Deliberately avoid a closed infinity sign, a circular chain badge, handshake, heart, wifi arcs, antenna, nodes/dots, mountain, location pin, speech bubble, recycling arrows, hexagon or hub-and-spoke network. One flat forest-green #164b32. Existing screenshot supplies only palette/restraint context; do not reproduce app screens or its current mountain icon. Presentation: large isolated new mark in upper half; spacious horizontal lockup below showing exact SAME geometry beside exact text "AuMaNET" in a carefully spaced humanist sans-serif; one small black specimen of identical mark near bottom. Only one symbol idea repeated; do not show alternative logo concepts. No headings/captions/slogan, no mockup scenery, no app UI, no card border/device, no gradients/shadows/texture/3D/photography. White negative space, polished professional identity design, simple 24px target and one-colour device-sticker potential.
